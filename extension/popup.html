Chitter

Catch every word. Keep what matters. Runs on your device.

Off by default. If on, Chrome sends call audio to Google for recognition. Chitter itself never sends anything anywhere.

Works on any site that plays sound, including Zoom in the browser, webinars and videos. Use it whenever captions don't start by themselves.

Starts by itself on Google Meet, Teams, Zoom (web), Webex, Whereby, Jitsi, Discord and Slack when a call connects. Tell people you're transcribing: laws vary.
